colon3 Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026

colon3 is a Soulseek client and music player for iPhone. It runs entirely on your phone. The developer runs no servers, has no accounts, collects no analytics and receives none of your data.

Google Drive

Backups are optional. If you connect Google Drive in Settings › Backup, colon3 asks for one permission: drive.file, which lets it see and change only the files it creates itself. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.

  • What colon3 stores in your Drive: a "colon3 Backup" folder holding colon3-backup.json (your song list, playlists, favourites, listening stats and app settings) and, if you turn on Include Music, copies of your song files with their covers and lyrics.
  • What colon3 reads from Google: the files above, to restore them, and your account's name and email address, shown in Settings so you know which account is connected.
  • Where it goes: straight between your phone and Google. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and none of it is used for advertising or to train AI models. The sign-in token is kept in your phone's Keychain.
  • Removing it: tap Disconnect in Settings › Backup, which signs out and revokes colon3's access, or remove colon3 at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Delete the "colon3 Backup" folder in Google Drive to delete the backup itself.

colon3's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other services colon3 talks to

Each of these is used only when you use the feature, directly from your phone:

  • Soulseek: your username, searches and shared music are visible to the Soulseek server and peers, as in any Soulseek client.
  • Last.fm: songs you play are scrobbled to your account if you log in, using your own API key.
  • Spotify and Apple Music: your playlists are read to import them, only when you ask.
  • YouTube: a playlist link you paste is loaded to read its song titles.
  • Apple (iTunes and charts), LRCLIB and MusicBrainz: song and album names are looked up for artwork, lyrics, tracklists and the Home charts.
  • GitHub: checked for app updates.

On your phone

Your music, playlists, stats and settings are stored in colon3's own storage on your phone. Passwords, tokens and API keys are kept in the Keychain and are never included in backups. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
